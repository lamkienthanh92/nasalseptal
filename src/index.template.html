Septal anatomy and the L-strut

The quadrilateral cartilage is the first-choice graft source in septorhinoplasty. A dorsal and a caudal strip (the L-strut) must stay behind to hold up the nose; what is left is the harvestable cartilage.

What the literature fixes

The strut is classically 10–15 mm wide. Finite-element work puts collapse risk up sharply once caudal-strut contact with the maxillary crest falls below about 45% [1]. Harvestable area after a 10 mm strut runs roughly 3.2–5.0 cm² across CT series, and falls with age as the cartilage ossifies [4,8].

What the app does

Align plane. The app samples the CT across the plane at every point and finds the thin tissue sheet with air on both sides (below −450 HU, 0.8 to 9 mm thick) closest to the plane. It fits a plane to those sheets, ignoring outliers, in three passes with a tightening gate of 12, 6 and 4 mm.

Suggest outline. The sheet counts as soft, meaning cartilage-like, where its highest HU is under 230. The largest soft region, cleaned by opening, becomes the outline. K is the top vertex, ASA the most anterior vertex above the lowest 15% of the outline, and ANS the lowest vertex at least a quarter of the width behind ASA. These are starting points to check and drag.

Measurements. Areas are projected on the plane; the curved-septum area adds the local slope of the sheet. Thickness includes mucosa and partial volume. Ossified share is the part of the outline whose sheet reaches 230 HU or more. Deviation is the offset of the sheet from the plane within the 5 mm gate.

L-strut. The dorsal strut runs K to ASA and the caudal strut ASA to ANS. Each line is moved inward by the strut width and the outline is cut by those two lines; what is left is the harvestable cartilage.

Plans. Grafts are rectangles tried at 7.5° steps on a 0.5 mm grid with about 1 mm between them. A plan fits only if every graft has its own space. Feasible means it still fits with the margin added, Borderline means it fits only at the listed sizes. The suggestion picks, among plans that fit, the one with the most grafts and then the least cartilage used.

Limits

This is a research and teaching tool, not a medical device. Every measurement and suggestion needs a clinician's check against the images.

The suggested outline is conservative. On four NasalSeg cases it overlapped an outline derived from the dataset's nasal-cavity labels by 0.4 to 0.7 (intersection over union) and was usually smaller, while the aligned plane was within 1.5° of the label-based plane. Septal cartilage is thin and close in density to mucosa, so always check and edit.

With 1.5 mm slices the vertical resolution of the reformat is 1.5 mm and thickness is only approximate. Septal extension and tympanoplasty sizes are placeholders, and the fit margin is a setting of this tool. Only axial scans aligned to the scanner axes are read, and DICOM must be uncompressed.
